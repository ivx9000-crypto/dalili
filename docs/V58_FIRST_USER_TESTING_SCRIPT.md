# Dalili First-User Testing Script

Use this script with 2–3 testers before wider sharing. Pick at least one person who has no M&E background.

## Test setup

- Use a small non-sensitive CSV or Excel file.
- Use a real-sounding project, but avoid live beneficiary records.
- Ask the tester to speak aloud as they use Dalili.

## Tasks

1. Create an account.
2. Create a new project.
3. Explain what Dalili is asking you to do next.
4. Open the Project Guide.
5. Upload a dataset in the Data Room.
6. Review the data dictionary and quality warning.
7. Open Track Results.
8. Choose one plain-language question.
9. Explain whether the result makes sense.
10. Generate or export a report/brief.
11. Log out and log back in.

## Observe

- Did the tester know the next step without help?
- Did the tester understand the words on the page?
- Did they know what a good final output was?
- Did they trust the result?
- Did any page crash or feel slow?

## Score each area from 1 to 5

- Clarity of next step
- Ease of upload
- Understanding of quality check
- Ease of Track Results
- Usefulness of report/export
- Overall confidence using Dalili without M&E staff

## Decision rule

Dalili is ready for a wider private test when at least 2 of 3 testers can complete the full journey without explanation from the product owner.
