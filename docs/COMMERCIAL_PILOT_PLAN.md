# Dalili Commercial Pilot Plan

## Objective
Validate whether Ugandan and East African M&E, research, and programme professionals understand Dalili, trust it, and would pay for it.

## Primary audiences to test
1. Independent M&E/evaluation consultants
2. NGO M&E officers and programme quality staff
3. Research firms and data managers
4. Donor-funded programme teams

## Demo workflow
1. Create/select a project
2. Load the sample dataset
3. Run backend DQA
4. Calculate an indicator
5. Review insight cards
6. Generate and export a report
7. Ask the AI Assistant for donor-report content
8. Capture structured feedback

## Questions to ask
- What part of this workflow would save you the most time?
- Which output would you pay for first?
- What would stop you from uploading real programme data?
- Who would approve purchase in your organisation?
- Which pricing model feels easiest: per project, monthly, annual, or bundled training?

## Early pricing hypotheses to test
- Consultant pilot: per project or per report pack
- NGO pilot: low monthly subscription
- Programme pilot: annual subscription with team users and governance settings

## Decision rule
After at least five demos, prioritise only the features that potential users say they would use in a real reporting workflow within 30 days.
